# Final Claims and Limitations

## Claims supported within scope

1. **A repair policy can reduce downstream decision regret relative to no repair under a controlled synthetic SCM contract.** Supported by Research C's frozen B=2 held-out primary, without extrapolation to manufacturing.
2. **Task-relevant structural information can be useful for prioritizing a limited repair budget.** Supported as a conditional conclusion across the C results and exploratory post-hoc analyses. It is not a universal guarantee.
3. **Repair value can depend on path availability, edge direction, graph constraints, target and candidate opportunity.** Supported by post-hoc mechanism evidence and objective sensitivity; not an identified causal decomposition.
4. **Budget increases can have non-monotonic mean benefit, and edit burden can change policy choice.** Supported by saved C B0–9 results and separate S4/S5 B0–4 re-aggregation.
5. **S5 provides conditional external semisynthetic evidence, with substantial uncertainty and a competitive graph-free baseline.** Do not state unqualified external superiority.
6. **S6 retrospective B=1 comparisons leave policy increments uncertain.** T5's replication point estimate was favorable versus Frozen M4, but its paired interval included zero and Holm-adjusted p was 1.0; Type-ranked M4 likewise did not establish added value.
7. **S7 establishes no external performance result.** It persisted no G0/recommendations; the separate task-zone feasibility is implementation-only, has an unresolved timing discrepancy, and static code review found a directed-edge orientation defect in the frozen learner-fitting path.

## Claims not supported

- M4 is best in every graph, budget, objective, cohort or cost regime.
- T5-guided M4 superiority or noninferiority is established.
- Structure repair generally beats graph-free learners.
- Perfect graph recovery guarantees optimal action or zero regret.
- A particular edit budget is universally optimal.
- Edge-edit count represents financial or staff cost savings.
- Results establish actual TEP/manufacturing intervention efficacy, safe deployment or field readiness.
- The full end-to-end combination of Fit-based verification, T5 repair, Adaptive repair, and Pareto stopping was tested as one algorithm.

## Essential conditions to repeat in presentations

Repair studies use reference DAG/task information. C and S4 are controlled synthetic SCMs, with S4 replication restricted to its Q-common-admitted strict-indirect population. D and S5 are conditional semisynthetic analyses; S5 uses one fitted DGP and 200 G0 draws. The historical D run and S5 are not pooled. E/F and supplementary analyses are post hoc. Confidence intervals are not all based on the same unit or uncertainty source. External generator-fit, source-data, training-frame variation, expert cost, and physical outcome uncertainty are not covered.
