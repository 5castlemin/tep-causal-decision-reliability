# S6 Candidate Opportunity and Redundancy

The reconstructed S4 Development cohort had initial legal M4 candidate counts `0:32, 1:25, 2:14, 3:6, 4:3`; 23/80 had at least two candidates. The 160-SCM replication cohort had `0:63, 1:56, 2:27, 3:8, 4:6`; 41/160 had at least two candidates. Consequently, 39/80 and 98/160 units respectively had no more than one candidate, limiting the chance to distinguish ranking rules.

In replication, the exact selected edit agreed in 151/160 SCMs between Frozen M4 and the Development-ranked type policy, and in 148/160 between Frozen M4 and T5. Each denominator includes the 63 SCMs where both policies chose no edit. Candidate scarcity, policy overlap, and identical actions can jointly yield a large zero mass. The 160-SCM cohort had already been used for prior S4 results, so this analysis is retrospective.
