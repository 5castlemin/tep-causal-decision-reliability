# S6-4 — Independent Validation Decision

**Status: `NOT_RUN`.** No new SCMs were generated.

The Development-selected error-type policy changed the B=1 choice in only a small fraction of SCMs relative to Frozen M4 and showed a small, uncertain mean difference in the already-used replication cohort. The saved records do not justify a fresh independent sample-size plan with a defensible minimum practically important difference; the S6 contrast's paired difference distribution also has high tie mass. The conditional confirmation gate therefore did not trigger. This is not a failed test or evidence that the policies are equivalent.

A future confirmatory study would need to define its target SCM population, a non-outcome-based structural opportunity rule, a meaningful regret difference, and a precision/power plan before generating observations. This document does not authorize that study.
