# Step 3 supplement: exploratory diagnostics

## Why the original direct pilot was all zero

The saved 114 rows were hash-verified before the supplement. The deleted A→M (S1) and A→M1 (S7) are upstream of the interventions M and M3. In the declared DAG, do(M) or do(M3) replaces the intervened equation and severs incoming paths, so these upstream edges do not affect the true intervention mean. This follows from the equations; the old raw record contains the original truth evaluations, not a second truth with that edge deleted. In the actual Ridge fits, restoring the upstream edge also leaves the target equation and all five predicted means and losses identical: S1 predictions range −0.54447 to 0.72197 and S7 −0.51102 to 1.10719. S1 selects k=+1, S7 k=−1; each is also the truth-grid optimum, hence regret 0. Thus the old result arose from intervention location and fitted equations, not merely the count of errors.

Of 456 runs, 208 had zero regret, 248 positive, none negative; 94 improved versus no-edit and none worsened. 248 had no action→target path. Prediction and recommendation both changed in 94 runs, and neither changed in the other 362; here no case had changed prediction but unchanged action. Across 96 same-condition/final-graph groups, recommended k and regret were identical. The raw results retain the five predicted means, losses, truth means and losses, edit ledgers, rejections, and costs.

With mixed errors at budget 2, there were 10 distinct final graphs per direct task and eight per path task; direct tasks had two distinct recommendations and path tasks one. At budget 4 both task types had two recommendations. Four of seven Global/Task-Focused pairs had different edit sequences for each direct task at B=2 or B=4; path-task pairs had identical sequences. These are counts among 19 policy/seed runs, not independent graph samples. In mixed conditions FN, FP and REV were executed 116, 84 and 76 times respectively.
