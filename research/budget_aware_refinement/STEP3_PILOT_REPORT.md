# Step 3 exploratory development pilot

The pre-run plan and hashes are in `STEP3_PILOT_PLAN.md` and `step3_results/manifest.json`. Two existing development structures S1 and S7, one direct task each, 256 newly generated observations per structure, one pre-existing remove-key graph variant, budgets 0/2/4, gamma=1 and all 14 policies plus three baseline kinds (random repeated with three seeds) produced 114/114 successful runs. Full candidate/selection/evaluation and graph ledgers, including failures had any occurred, are in `step3_results/raw.json`; summary is `step3_results/summary.json`. These are exploratory samples, not independent SCMs.

All 114 regrets were zero and no recommendation changed versus no-edit. At B=0 each structure produced one final graph; at B=2 and 4 there were two each. At most one cost unit was spent: the chosen controlled graph has a single FN. Consequently this pilot does not distinguish the 14 policies, evaluate FP/REV opportunities, establish a useful budget grid, estimate generalization or justify policy superiority. The direct task was fixed before execution and is not changed in response. Path F1 is an oracle with extra reference-path information; its output is not equally informed with G/T. Runtime and selection logs are in raw results; no runtime extrapolation or power estimate is supported by two SCMs.

Independent analytic test: A→B→Y coefficients 2 and 3 gives E[Y|do(A=2)]=12; tested against the existing analytic evaluator. The learned response uses a different fitted model. The six adapter tests exercise the real call flow, truth perturbation, learner input boundary, schema rejection, and no-path tie. They do not constitute a security sandbox or prove causal identification. No intervention experiment on TEP or CausalMan was run. Before registration, review controlled multi-error designs, task choice without outcome selection, independent SCM sample size, minimal meaningful difference, uncertainty, failure rules and Path F1 computational scaling. CI and complete reproducibility checks remain a completion gate.
