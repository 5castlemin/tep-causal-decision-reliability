# Proposed failure contract — not yet frozen

2026-11-10; base `42b7c2a0e9d6c11ed72aae4205c0bb7114221a2a`; source hash in readiness audit. Every planned SCM, task, graph, policy and random seed receives one immutable status and denominator. Separate structure-ineligible and bounded graph-generation failures from learner, recommendation, truth-evaluator, nonfinite, timeout and system failures. Log attempted seeds/reasons. A cycle-rejected edit, no permitted candidate, budget exhaustion, no-edit, or unused budget is a valid outcome, not a failed run; keep the resulting graph and cost.

Proposed handling: when training data and GT evaluation are valid but the learner or recommender fails, preserve the original failure and evaluate a separately labelled k=0 fallback using the pre-existing candidate set; report intention-to-handle fallback regret and failure rate. A fallback is a decision rule, never relabelled as successful learner output. If training scale/action candidate or GT response is invalid, k=0 cannot be assumed meaningful: do not assign an arbitrary penalty; retain missing outcome, reason, the complete denominator and success-conditional paired analysis with selection-bias limitations. Shared data/GT failures affect all policies in that cell; policy-specific failures must not disappear in a complete-case mean. No automatic retries except predeclared infrastructure retry with identical input/seed and preservation of all attempts.

Still unresolved before freezing: maximum allowed missingness and explicit stop rule for confirmation, structural rejection/replacement maximum, whether fallback policy is the primary estimand or sensitivity analysis, and what to do when a candidate policy has missing cells during development selection. A numerical penalty or failure threshold is not silently invented from pilot success. These unresolved choices block Step4 DONE.
