# Factorial contribution and metric complementarity

The corrected cohort-aggregate table includes a descriptive marginal-contribution surface for each nonempty subset J and added metric Tj, comparing mean regret for J with mean regret for J+Tj at identical cohort, budget, and source sample. Results are summarized by parent-set size in `tables/correction_v1/descriptive_metric_marginal_contributions.csv`. The contrast changes the equal-weight average of structural gains and is therefore not an isolated causal effect of a metric.

The aggregate surface contains dependent comparisons and does not provide SCM-paired uncertainty, full trajectory equivalence clusters, or sequence-interaction tests. Formal complementarity claims for T3/T4, T3/T5, T3/T6, T1/T2 and edit-order interactions remain unverified. The ex-post two-edit interaction analysis was not run.
