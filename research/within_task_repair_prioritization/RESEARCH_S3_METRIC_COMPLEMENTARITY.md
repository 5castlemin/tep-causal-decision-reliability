# Metric overlap and complementarity

The 63-subset aggregate surface is provided in `tables/s3_policy_aggregates.csv`. At a fixed SCM and budget, different subsets frequently produced the same final graph; `unique_final_graphs` reports the number of distinct final graphs within each cohort/policy/budget, and first-edit disagreement with M4 is also reported.

A complete factorial paired marginal-contribution table, candidate-rank correlation matrix, and complete trajectory-equivalence clustering were not produced. Therefore this report does not claim that any metric has a unique or general marginal causal contribution. Averaging component gains changes relative weights as well as adding information.
