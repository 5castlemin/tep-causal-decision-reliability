# Paper contribution guide

Defensible contribution: a decision-oriented framing that distinguishes global graph accuracy from task-specific structural criteria and demonstrates, exploratorily, that repair value can vary within the same M4 error class. The work also provides a concrete family of graph-only T1–T6 prioritization scores and a bounded lookahead diagnostic.

Do not claim novelty as first-ever, superiority to state of the art, confirmatory validation, industry impact, or a validated combined verification-and-repair algorithm. The contribution is methodological and empirical within the frozen synthetic Research C setup; S3 is post-hoc.
