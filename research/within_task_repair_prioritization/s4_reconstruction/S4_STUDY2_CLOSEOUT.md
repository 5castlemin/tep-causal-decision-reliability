# Study 2 Closeout — S4 reconstruction

## Evidence status

The existing S4 pilot (64 SCMs), reconstructed development (80 SCMs), and newly seeded replication (160 SCMs) are separate cohorts. The historical other-PC S4 results remain unverified. The new replication is a post-hoc reconstruction/replication of the T5 candidate, not a recovery of the missing S4-3 execution.

## Replication primary

At B=1 and gamma=0, paired `T5_GUIDED_M4 − Frozen M4` mean regret difference was −0.0086644 (median 0; 95% SCM bootstrap interval [−0.0203910, 0.0000000]). T5 had lower regret on 4/160 units, higher on 1/160, and tied on 155/160. The half-width (.01020) narrowly missed the .010 precision target. Superiority and noninferiority were not established. Mean edit counts were equal (.60625); final graph agreement was 148/160 and action agreement 155/160.

The primary denominator retains all units, including no-candidate cases. Q-common admission defines the conditional target population. See `S4_REPLICATION_PRIMARY_RESULTS.md` and `outputs/confirmation_v1/`.

## Interpretation

The result is compatible with a small T5 benefit and with no mean difference. The high tie mass and limited candidate opportunity constrain what this cohort can say about ranking. It does not establish a general priority rule, and it is not evidence from an independent external environment.
