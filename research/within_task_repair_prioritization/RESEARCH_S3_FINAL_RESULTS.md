# S3 final results

The exploratory feasibility gate passed: 11/64 Development and 35/200 Held-out SCMs had at least two initially legal M4 candidates, and T3/full-six policies differed from M4 in first edit among some comparable SCMs. All 63 greedy combinations were calculated for B=1–4; 13 lookahead combinations for B=2. New independent data generation remained zero.

The M4 vs expected random-M4 paired mean difference (M4 minus random expectation) was -0.001068 in Development (95% interval [-0.019703, 0.019316]) and -0.000745 in Held-out ([-0.010052, 0.008663]). These intervals include zero; the mean differences are small.

At B=1, six-metric Greedy was lower than M4 in both cohorts, but the contrast is post-hoc and no multiplicity-adjusted claim is made. At B=2 Held-out, T4+T5/T5 had the lowest observed mean. Across lookahead comparisons, only T3+T4 showed a nonzero Held-out paired result (1/200 SCM; interval touches zero). See aggregate CSVs and the detailed limitations.
