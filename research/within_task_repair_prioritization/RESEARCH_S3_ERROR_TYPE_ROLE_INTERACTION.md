# Error type × structural role

Path and backdoor labels are assigned from the reference graph; role categories are mutually exclusive for this table (`path_and_backdoor`, `path`, `backdoor`, `other_task_fp`, `other_task`) to prevent double-counting. Candidate counts and represented SCM counts are both shown. FP candidates in this pool are Rule A task false positives. Structural combinations that cannot arise are retained with zero support.

SCM-level mean one-edit regret gains and clustered intervals are in `tables/s3_candidate_value_aggregates.csv`; opportunity counts are in `tables/s3_error_role_counts.csv`. Sparse cells preclude stable ranking. These are observational post-hoc comparisons among different graph errors, not estimates of a causal effect of assigning error type.
