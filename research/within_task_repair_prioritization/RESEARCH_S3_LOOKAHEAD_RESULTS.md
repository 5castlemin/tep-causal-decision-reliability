# Two-step lookahead

Lookahead was limited to 13 subsets, with B=2. Full greedy B=2 acts as the two-edit one-step comparator. Six singleton scores, the all-six subset and six specified pairs were included. The common-index intervals for every lookahead-vs-greedy comparison are in `tables/s3_primary_paired_intervals.csv`.

For T4, B=2 mean regret was 0.002926 in Held-out for both lookahead and greedy; all 200 were tied. For T3+T4, lookahead mean regret was 0.002790 versus greedy 0.003717; paired mean difference (lookahead minus greedy) was -0.000928, 95% pointwise interval [-0.002783, 0], with 1 better and 0 worse SCMs (199 ties). This is a rare-change exploratory result, not robust superiority. The exhaustive 63-subset lookahead and runtime/candidate-count instrumentation were not executed.
