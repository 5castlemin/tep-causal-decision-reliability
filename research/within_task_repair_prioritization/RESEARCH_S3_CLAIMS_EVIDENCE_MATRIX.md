# S3 claims and evidence

| Claim | Evidence grade | Permitted wording | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Value differs among M4 errors | Post-hoc mechanism evidence | “Observed one-edit regret gains varied across candidate errors and structural roles in the original C cohorts.” | Candidate selection was not randomized; sparse cells. |
| Structural metrics can select differently | Descriptive | “T3 and six-metric Greedy selected a different first edit from M4 in some eligible SCMs.” | This is not evidence of better decisions or external generalization. |
| Metric Greedy beats M4 | Unsupported as a general claim | Report cohort-specific means and exploratory intervals. | post-hoc, many combinations, no Holm correction. |
| Lookahead improves outcomes | Unsupported generally | “T3+T4 differed from its greedy counterpart in one Held-out SCM.” | 1/200; pointwise interval touches zero; 13 lookahead sets only. |
| A type or role causes value | Unsupported | Do not use causal wording. | Nonrandom errors and heterogeneous SCM contexts. |
| Results transfer to D/industry | Unsupported | No transfer claim. | D unit-level data unavailable; synthetic C only. |
