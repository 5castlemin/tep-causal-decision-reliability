# Critical review

**What the data support:** M4 contains multiple legal edit opportunities in enough original SCMs for a bounded exploratory comparison; structural criteria sometimes select a different first edit; observed regret varies by candidate and role; the held-out ranking across metrics differs from Development; the T3+T4 lookahead signal is concentrated in one held-out SCM.

**What they do not support:** confirmatory evidence, a universal best metric, generalization to unseen SCMs, error-type causal effects, full sequence synergy, a deployment-ready repair policy, a validated Research D extension, or actual industrial cost savings. The analysis was run after prior C Held-out results had been observed and before S3 code was committed.

Research C's frozen primary remains intact. Research D remains one fixed semisynthetic DGP and was not expanded. Additional S3 analyses were stopped at the documented scope rather than repeatedly searching for favorable combinations.
