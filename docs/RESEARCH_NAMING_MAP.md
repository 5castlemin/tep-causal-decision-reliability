## Core narrative

Research 0 establishes why structural accuracy alone may not characterize decision value. Research 1 asks **where** limited repair effort should go. Research 2 asks **what** to prioritize inside task-relevant structure and **why**, including whether detailed metrics add reliable value. Research 3 examines what carries over to stated semisynthetic environments, while keeping S7's computational feasibility separate from external performance. Research 4 asks **how much** repair is worthwhile under observed edit-count and objective trade-offs.
