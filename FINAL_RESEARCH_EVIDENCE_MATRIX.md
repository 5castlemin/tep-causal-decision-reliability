## Citation and reporting rule

For every presentation estimate, report study/cohort, analysis unit, budget, objective, contrast direction, scale, CI type, and evidence class. S5's G0-only intervals are not two-source intervals; C/S4 regret and S5 tracking loss are not on a common scale. S4 metric/candidate rows and S5 response rows remain private; public citations point to aggregate artifacts only.
